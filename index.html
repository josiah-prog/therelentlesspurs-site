About me

I’m a former college athlete, runner, and writer with a deep fascination for what people are capable of becoming.

I’ve spent a lot of my life drawn to the pursuit of something difficult. the kind of pursuit that demands more of you than you thought you had to give, and somehow makes you love being alive a little more.

I write about the things I find myself coming back to: belief, ambition, the human spirit, and the relentless pursuit of a life worth living.

mostly, I’m just trying to make the most of my time here, and hopefully encourage a few people to do the same.

- jo johnson
